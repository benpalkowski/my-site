The Trump Administration has made a Trump Accounts App available for download. The app is reportedly used to register and activate a Trump Account, and to monitor investments and manage contributions. 

According to [an annoucment by the Trasury Department](https://www.whitehouse.gov/videos/download-trump-accounts-app-now/), the Trump Accounts app will be the main interface for managing these accounts.

The Trump Accoutns were introduced last year as part of the so-called "One Big Beautiful Bill Act." The legislation established tax-advanteded investment accounts for children, with the federal covernment seeding accounts for children bord between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2028.
